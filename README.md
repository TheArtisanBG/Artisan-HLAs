This mod is an ongoing project to rework HLAs. The mod requires EEex to function.

Current HLA additions/changes:

# Universal HLAs
*These HLAs are available for all classes and most kits.*

## Epic Prowess
The character's skill at combat grows, permanently granting them a +1 bonus to hit and damage. This ability may be selected once.
## Armor Skin
The character's armor class vs. all weapon types permanently increases by 1. This ability may be selected once.
## Epic Marksman
The character permanently gains a +1 damage bonus on ranged attacks. Depending on the character's Dexterity, they gain a further +1 bonus at 19, 22 and 25 Dexterity respectively. This ability may be selected once. Requires: Epic Prowess, Proficiency in Crossbow, Longbow, Shortbow, Dart or Sling
## Epic Toughness
The character permanently gains an additional +15 Maximum Hit Points. This ability may be selected up to ten times.
## Improved Critical
The character permanently gains a +1 bonus to their critical threat range. This ability may be selected once.
## Epic Reflexes
The character permanently gains a +1 bonus to Saving Throws vs. Breath and Polymorph. This ability may be selected up to three times.
## Epic Fortitude
The character permanently gains a +1 bonus to Saving Throws vs. Death. This ability may be selected up to three times.
## Epic Will
The character permanently gains a +1 bonus to Saving Throws vs. Spells and Wands. This ability may be selected up to three times.
## Epic Weapon Focus (requires EEex proficiency system applied to Artisan's Kitpack)
The character may gain a proficiency point in any chosen weapon type. If a weapon that the character can not normally be wielded is chosen, they gain the ability to wield that weapon. This ability may be selected up to three times, but may only be used once per weapon type.

# Warrior HLAs
*These HLAs are available for warrior classes including fighters, rangers and paladins. Some may be available for specific kits.*

## Blinding Speed
This ability allows the user to move and attack at extraordiary speed. For the next two turns, the user's movement rate is quadrupled, and their attacks per rounds are doubled as per an Improved Haste spell. As this effect is not truly magical in nature, it may not be dispelled as Improved Haste might. This ability may be selected once. Upon selecting this ability, it may be used twice per day.

## Whirlwind Attack
This ability allows the warrior to unleash a flurry of super-fast blows. For the duration, the user's THAC0 is penalized by 4 points and they may not make critical hits, but are Hasted for the duration and each attack made will roll to hit twice. The additional attack deals 50% reduced physical damage. The Whirlwind Attack lasts for one round. Requires: Blinding Speed

## Critical Strike
Upon using this ability, the warrior unleashes a chain of increasingly lethal strikes. For the next three successful melee attacks after landing this ability, the warrior immediately attacks again with an additional hit that will always critically strike. The second and third additional attacks deal 25% and 50% increased physical damage respectively, and the final attack always deals maximum damage. Requires: Improved Critical

## Sundering Strike (replacing Power Attack)
This powerful two-strike technique allows warriors of great strength to break their opponent's guard and then inflict a debilitating attack. After this ability is used, the first melee attack made by the user Slows the target, reducing their Armor Class and THAC0 by 4, and reduces their physical resistances by a value equal to the user's Strength stat multiplied by 2 for three rounds. The second attack made afterwards has an increased melee damage modifier of 10 and Stuns the target for two rounds unless a Saving Throw vs. Death at -2 is made, with an additional -1 modifier per point of Strength above 18. When Sundering Strike is used, the warrior may only make a maximum of two attacks per round until both attacks are made.

## Hardiness
Upon selecting this ability for the first time, the warrior permanently gains 10% resistance to all forms of physical damage.
When used as a special ability, the warrior calls upon hidden reserves of strength during times of danger, gaining 10% resistance to all physical damage plus 1% additional resistance for every 10 Maximum Hit Points they have at the time of casting, up to a maximum of 40% total at 300 Maximum Hit Points. The ability lasts for 1 round for every 2 levels of the warrior. This ability does not stack with Defensive Stance. Requires: Epic Toughness

## One Shot (replacing War Cry)
The user can attempt to deliver an extremely powerful ranged attack at the cost of any additional attacks made for the next round. This attack is a guaranteed critical hit and inflicts additional non-mitigated missile damage equal to double the user's level. This ability may only be used once per turn. Requires: Epic Marksman

## Last Stand (replacing Smite)
The user gains a +150 temporary bonus to their maximum hit points for the next turn and immunity to all movement-impairing and mind-affecting effects. However, they may not receive healing from most sources while under the effects. When the duration ends, they lose the temporary hit points and take 150 points of non-lethal damage, knocking them unconscious if they would fall below one hit point. Requires: Epic Toughness, Hardiness
